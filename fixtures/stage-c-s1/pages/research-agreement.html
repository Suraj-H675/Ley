Local research use agreement

A fictional agreement for collaborators who request a copy of an Asteria observation record.

Request context

A collaborator names the session IDs, intended analysis, and requested date range. The station lead checks that the request identifies a record the archive actually holds. A broad request receives a follow-up question before a volunteer prepares a copy.

Citation and limits

A shared copy retains the record ID and revision date. A report states which sessions it used and which measurements were unavailable. The agreement does not claim that a fixture observation is real-world evidence; all records in this example belong to the fictional Asteria scenario.
