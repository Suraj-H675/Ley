Asteria Ridge Observatory field index

A working index for the fictional Asteria Ridge community observatory, its instruments, records, and operating procedures.

What this site covers

Asteria Ridge is a small fictional observatory maintained by a rotating team of local volunteers. The pages describe its field equipment and records as a self-contained example corpus. Instrument values and identifiers belong to this scenario; they do not describe a real facility or prescribe real safety practice.

How the pages fit together

The station charter explains responsibilities, while the site layout and network map locate equipment. Field logs connect measurements to calibration notes and export formats. The radio handbook covers the control link. Maintenance and safety pages describe the handoffs that keep a night session traceable.

Finding a record

Use the identifier registry when a label is unfamiliar. The archive page and research agreement are intentionally not linked from this index or from any other page. A separate extra-seed profile can request them directly, which makes them useful for measuring the difference between links and supplied URLs.
