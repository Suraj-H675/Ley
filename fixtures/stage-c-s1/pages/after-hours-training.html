After-hours visitor training note

A fictional internal training note about visitor orientation during an evening session.

Before entering the yard

A visitor waits for the lead and stays on the marked path. The lead names the active equipment and explains which areas are closed during observations. A visitor does not handle a device or disconnect a cable unless the local supervisor explicitly authorizes that work.

During a demonstration

The lead explains the displayed value and its unit before showing a live reading. The visitor may ask questions, but the recorder keeps the session log separate from informal discussion. If the demonstration changes a camera exposure or sensor position, the lead records the change as an event.

Scope of this note
