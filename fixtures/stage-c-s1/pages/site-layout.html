Asteria Ridge site layout

A route description for the fictional instrument yard and the small operations room.

Instrument yard

The north pad holds the weather mast and rain gauge. The west pier carries the wind vane and air-quality inlet. A covered enclosure on the east side protects the sky camera from direct lamp glare. The labels describe the fictional site and do not give directions to a real location.

Operations room

The operations room contains the radio console, time display, and a read-only copy of the network map. The backup battery cabinet sits outside the room beside the marked disconnect. During a session, the equipment log records which console and port were used, not only the instrument name.

Moving equipment

A moved instrument receives a new location note before its next reading. The recorder uses the identifier registry to keep the device identity stable while its site position changes. For electrical work, the crew follows the power-system page and the local safety plan rather than treating the layout sketch as an isolation procedure.
