Weather mast sensor notes

The weather mast records air temperature and relative humidity at a fixed height above the pad.

Mounting

The fictional mast label is `WX-04`. Its temperature shield sits 1.8 m above the north pad surface, away from the operations-room exhaust. The log records the height because moving the probe closer to the ground would change what the reading represents.

Reading checks

Before each session, the crew confirms that the shield is clear and the probe cable is dry. The display reports degrees Celsius and percent relative humidity. If the reported value is frozen while the display clock advances, the crew records a sensor-status fault rather than copying the same value as a sequence of new observations.

Calibration link

The crew compares the mast probe with the reference thermometer during scheduled maintenance. A calibration entry names both device IDs and the ambient conditions. The rain gauge and wind vane have separate checks because a shared visit does not make their measurement methods interchangeable.
