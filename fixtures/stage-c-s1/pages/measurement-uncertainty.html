Reporting measurement uncertainty

The guide separates display resolution from observed variation in repeated measurements.

Resolution

A display that changes in steps of 0.1 °C has a resolution of 0.1 °C. That step size does not say that the measurement is accurate to 0.1 °C. The calibration record names the reference check and conditions that support any accuracy statement.

Repeat readings

When a crew takes repeated readings, the log preserves each value and the interval between them. The summary may report the minimum and maximum as an observed span, but it names the sample count. Three readings over one minute do not describe variation over a full night.

Avoid false precision

If the instrument reports one decimal place, the export does not add extra zeros to imply finer precision. A derived average can retain additional digits internally, but the displayed result follows the documented rounding rule and identifies the input samples. Missing readings stay missing rather than being filled with a neighboring value.
