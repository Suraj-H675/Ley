Reading the station pages with assistive technology

The page conventions keep instrument names and measurement context readable in a linear view.

Page structure

Each page begins with one heading that names its subject. Section headings describe the information that follows, and links use the destination title rather than a bare instruction such as “click here.” A screen reader can move through the heading list without needing to infer a page topic from an icon.

Measurement text

A table or chart has a text explanation of its units and time range. The recorder expands an unfamiliar abbreviation at first use and keeps the device ID in the same sentence as the reading. Color alone does not indicate whether a value is missing or out of range.

Reviewing changes

When a page changes, the revision note describes the changed field in words. The station charter asks the shift recorder to keep corrections additive. These conventions help a reader compare an observation and its source without relying on a particular display size or color setting.
