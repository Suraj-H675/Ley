Routine maintenance schedule

A schedule for recurring checks and their evidence records at the fictional observatory.

Weekly checks

The weekly visit checks cable strain, enclosure seals, the weather shield, and the rain gauge funnel. The checklist names the instrument ID and the observed condition. A box marked complete without a note cannot show whether the crew inspected the right device.

Monthly checks

The monthly visit verifies clock agreement, confirms a backup restore, and reviews calibration dates. The maintenance record lists the person who completed each action and the evidence file created. If a check cannot run, the schedule carries the open item to the next shift instead of silently resetting its due date.

Repairs

A repair that changes wiring or mount orientation gets a new event in the field log. The crew follows the power-system disconnect procedure before opening a powered enclosure. The backup procedure protects session files during the repair, but it does not replace the original archive copy.
