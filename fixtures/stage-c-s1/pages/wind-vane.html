Wind vane orientation and readings

The wind vane record defines the direction convention used by the Asteria field log.

Direction convention

The display reports the direction from which the wind arrives, measured clockwise from north. A reading of 090° means wind from the east. The log keeps degrees as a numeric value and records the averaging interval, because an instantaneous direction can move sharply even when the interval mean is steady.

Alignment check

A painted north mark on the mount is checked against the site reference after any maintenance. The crew records the vane ID `WV-03`, the alignment result, and any obstruction in the nearby yard. A mast rotated by one quarter turn can produce plausible values with the wrong orientation.

Reading limits

The vane does not measure wind speed. The weather mast page describes the separate sensor used for temperature and humidity. If ice restricts movement, the recorder marks direction as unavailable and describes the obstruction rather than reporting the last displayed angle as current.
