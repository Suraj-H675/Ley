After-hours visitor training note, revised

This revised note moves visitor orientation to a supervised daytime briefing. The change follows a fictional station review and applies to later Asteria sessions.

Briefing requirement

The lead gives visitors a daytime explanation of the marked path, active equipment, and closed areas. Visitors wait for the assigned supervisor before entering the instrument yard. They do not operate or disconnect equipment.

Session record

The recorder records that the briefing occurred and identifies the session lead. Questions stay outside the observation log unless they cause an equipment change or interrupt sampling. Any such change receives a dated event note.
