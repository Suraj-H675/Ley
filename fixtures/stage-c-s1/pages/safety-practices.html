General session safety practices

These fictional station notes direct volunteers to local facility rules and define when to stop work.

Stop conditions

A volunteer stops a task when a cable is damaged, a cover is wet, an odor is unexplained, or the work area differs from the local plan. The volunteer moves to the designated safe area and contacts the session lead. This corpus does not replace a site-specific risk assessment or training.

Record the event

The lead identifies which equipment was involved and whether observations stopped. The recorder preserves the session file and writes the event time, people notified, and immediate action taken. The note describes what was observed and avoids assigning a cause before a qualified review.

Power and maintenance

Only authorized personnel handle electrical enclosures. The power-system overview is informational, not a lockout procedure. The maintenance schedule assigns a qualified person to complete equipment checks under the relevant local rules.
