Air-quality inlet handling

The fictional particle monitor uses a clear inlet and a documented warm-up interval.

Inlet placement

The particle monitor `AQ-11` draws air through a short inlet above the west pad. The opening points down to reduce direct rain entry. The crew checks that a cable tie or maintenance cover has not blocked the opening before trusting a low particle count.

Warm-up and sample

After power-up, the monitor needs five minutes to stabilize. The field log records power-on time and the first accepted sample time. A zero value during warm-up is not filed as evidence of clean air. The page describes a fixture instrument for this corpus, not a validated environmental monitor.

Cross-check

A calibration visit compares the monitor against its reference unit and records both serial IDs. The crew notes dust events and nearby work because particles can rise for reasons unrelated to the inlet. A change in displayed units receives a new export note so old rows are not misread.
