Station charter and shift roles

The Asteria Ridge charter defines who records a session and how a shift hands work to the next crew.

Session ownership

Each observing session has one named lead and one recorder. The lead confirms the instrument list before the first sample. The recorder writes the session identifier, start time, weather note, and any interruptions into the field log. A visitor may contribute an observation, but the recorder keeps the original entry and adds the visitor as its source.

Handoffs

A shift handoff includes unfinished checks, equipment left disconnected, and files that have not reached the archive. The incoming crew reads the last field log entry aloud and checks the maintenance schedule. If the outgoing lead cannot attend, the recorder marks the handoff as remote and identifies the person who accepted responsibility.

Corrections

A correction never erases the first account. The recorder adds a dated note, cites the affected record ID, and explains why the value changed. The incident reporting page uses the same method for safety events, so a later reader can distinguish a correction from an original measurement.
