Sky camera capture notes

The sky camera records the visible cloud field with a fixed exposure and a session timestamp.

Capture settings

The fictional camera `CAM-05` records one frame every five minutes with a fixed exposure. The recorder logs any manual exposure change as a separate event. Without that note, a brightness change could be mistaken for a change in sky conditions.

Time and orientation

The frame timestamp comes from the camera clock and is compared with the session display at the start of the night. The camera points toward the northern horizon and includes a small orientation marker. The clock-sync page describes the comparison method.

Use limits

A frame can show cloud cover or a bright obstruction, but it cannot identify every source of reflected light. The operations light is switched off before a capture run. The night-operations page describes when the crew reviews frames, while the site layout shows the camera enclosure location.
