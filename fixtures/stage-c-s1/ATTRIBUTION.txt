Asteria Ridge Observatory fixture corpus

The original HTML text, route descriptions, and fixture metadata in this directory were created for the Ley Stage C controlled-collection example. Asteria Ridge, its staff, devices, records, and operating details are fictional. The documents do not describe a real observatory or provide real-world safety instructions.
